Focus Pomodoro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Focus Pomodoro is a study timer app. Your privacy is simple to explain, because the app collects nothing.

Data collection

  • The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Your tasks, settings, and study statistics are stored only on your device (in the app's local storage). They never leave your phone.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.
  • The app does not require an account and works fully offline.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to alert you when a focus session or break ends. You can decline or revoke this permission at any time in your system settings; the timer still works without it.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data, since everything is stored locally on your device.
